Hands-on management, backed by our own investment.

Acceleron Investments UK Limited (AIL) is a UK-based management consultancy, registered in England and Wales since November 2022. We provide strategic, financial and managerial support to UK companies, including those our shareholders invest in, so they can overcome challenges, improve performance and reach lasting profitability.

Who we help

We work with start-ups and with existing companies that are challenged in their results, across a range of industry sectors. What they have in common is a need for practical, effective and modern management.

Our work is aimed at four outcomes:

  • Organisational improvements that make the company easier to run
  • The company's mission and goals delivered on time
  • Higher profitability
  • A better return for investors

Why we invest in our clients

What sets us apart is that we invest in the businesses we consult. Our success is directly linked to theirs. We focus on building and growing sustainable enterprises, not on collecting fees.

The management team also takes on only projects it finds genuinely interesting.

Where our experience comes from

Our strength is hands-on management experience, deep industry insight and a global perspective built across many countries and sectors, from single projects to large companies running many projects in many countries.

Our independent team brings together professionals with experience in:

  • United Kingdom
  • Africa
  • USA
  • India
  • Poland
  • China

We develop projects at every level of the organisation, from the board and executive team to strategic management, so growth is sustainable rather than dependent on one person.

Sectors we know

Projects our team has led or managed include:

  • Hospitality services, facilities management and catering
  • Accommodation and camps for mining clients
  • Hotels, including fast-build prefabricated hotels
  • Fashion production and retail
  • Corporate travel management
  • Calibration gases and gas detection

Leaving places better

As part of our commitment to the environments we operate in, our designs account for energy and water saving measures and techniques, and for the greatest possible community engagement.

Our team has developed and run social responsibility programmes in 12 African countries, from first-job training to exclusive local hiring. That work included health and safety guidance for local producers and sourcing high-quality local produce, which now generates USD 8.3 million in annual turnover.
