Operators, not observers.

The people who work on your business have managed companies themselves, in the UK and in many countries abroad.

Sanjay Narain

Founder and CEO

Sanjay founded the company and leads its direction. He has management experience across more than 30 countries and a strong grounding in international trade, management strategy and operational control. His early focus was hospitality; his career has since expanded into technology, fashion and retail.

His most significant investment was Allterrain Services (ATS). As Group CEO he grew it from a single-country operation in 1998 into a leading hospitality services company in 22 countries, with over 100 project sites, 4,500 employees and USD 150 million in annual sales. He oversaw operations, product development, IT, finance, HR and compliance across multiple languages and tax systems, and secured contracts worth over USD 1 billion.

In 2017 he exited ATS, returning more than 60 times the initial investment to all shareholders. Today he is focused on raising the standard of hospitality and on international ventures in gas sensor technology and business innovation.

  • First-class honours, Hospitality Management, South Bank University, London
  • 30+ countries

Danijela Panic

Executive Director

Danijela has more than 25 years of international business management experience, from building investor relations for gold exploration companies in Canada to pioneering hospitality services across Africa. She has conceived and delivered hospitality solutions in more than 12 countries.

She leads the design teams in businesses that need creative direction, including hotels and fashion, and has introduced strategic design management together with improvements in marketing, communications and customer service. She works closely with tradespeople, local artisans and professionals to bring aesthetic and cultural value to each project, while keeping it commercially successful.

  • BA Economics
  • 25+ years
  • 12+ countries

Matija Dragutinovic

Operational Manager

Matija oversees operations across the group's businesses in the United Kingdom, Kenya and Serbia, bringing business systems, finance and automation together so each company runs on clear, reliable routines.

A large part of that work is putting AI to practical use: AI tools take over repetitive preparation and reporting, so the teams spend their time on decisions rather than paperwork, and every important decision stays with people.

Before this, Matija spent more than a decade in sales and logistics and for eight years led an independent e-commerce company, with hands-on experience in websites and financial management.

  • Operations
  • Business systems
  • AI in operations
  • Finance and automation
  • UK, Kenya, Serbia
