Earlier projects

1998 – 2017

Allterrain Services (ATS)

A pan-African facilities management and catering company and the largest project our key people have managed. As Group CEO, Sanjay Narain grew ATS from one country to a market leader in its region, with over 100 project sites and USD 150 million in annual sales.

He developed every part of the company, including operations, products, IT, finance, HR and management, across multiple languages and tax environments, and secured more than USD 1 billion in client contracts.

ATS was founded in Ghana and grew into sub-Saharan Africa, with operations in countries including Sierra Leone, Mali, Senegal, Burkina Faso, the DRC, Zambia, Tanzania, Uganda, Kenya and Mozambique. Mining clients included Barrick (Lumwana, Zambia), Randgold Resources and Banro. In March 2015 ATS merged with Tsebo Outsourcing Group, and Sanjay remained ATS CEO within the group.
