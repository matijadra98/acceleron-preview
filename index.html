What we fix

We start at the top: the plan, the numbers and the way decisions get made. Then we stay until the changes hold.

Strategy and the board

We review strategic planning with the board and executive team and turn it into priorities, owners and dates that deliver the company's mission and goals on time.

  • Strategic review
  • Board and executive priorities
  • Delivery plan

Financial performance and cash flow

We look at where the business makes and loses money, secure cash flow and set up the reporting management needs to act early, with the aim of higher profitability and a better return for investors.

  • Profitability review
  • Cash flow control
  • Management reporting

Management and operations

We simplify complex decision-making and fix management processes, so the company can act faster. When a hard decision is needed, we make it and move the business forward.

  • Management processes
  • Operational control
  • Organisational improvement

Growth in world markets

  • Multi-country operations

How we work

We stay focused on what truly drives a business and act on problems quickly, without getting lost in unnecessary detail.

“We stay focused on what truly drives a business: ensuring cash flow, powering growth, and making decisive, informed choices.”

Sanjay NarainCEO, Acceleron Investments

  1. Review from the top

    Strategic planning, financial performance and management processes, at board, executive and management level.

  2. Decide and simplify

    We cut complex decisions down to what matters and agree the actions with the people who will carry them out.

  3. Act on the hard calls

    When something is holding the business back, we resolve it, even when the decision is a tough one.

  4. Invest alongside you

    We invest in the businesses we consult. Our success is directly linked to yours, so we build for the long term.

From one country to a pan-African leader

Allterrain Services (ATS) is a facilities management and catering company. As Group CEO, Sanjay Narain grew it from one country in 1998 into a leading hospitality services company across Africa, until ATS merged with Tsebo Outsourcing Group in March 2015. He stayed on as CEO of ATS within the group before exiting.

He ran every part of that growth: operations, products, IT, finance, HR and compliance, across many languages and tax systems.
